\begin{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{Background.} Fisher--Kolmogorov--Petrovskii--Piskunov (Fisher--KPP) models describe glioma spread and growth but do not explicitly represent oxygen limitation or necrotic material. We developed a minimal coupled extension and an interactive workflow to examine these assumptions.\par
\noindent\textbf{Methods.} Tissue-dependent Fisher--KPP, oxygen-limited growth and viable-tumor--oxygen--necrosis dynamics were compared on BrainWeb over 90 days with assigned parameters. Analytical checks, ablations and refinement assessed the numerics. Three synthetic experiments estimated homogeneous diffusion/proliferation, separate BrainWeb white-/gray-matter diffusion with shared proliferation, and those three rates plus anisotropy on real sub-PAT16 anatomy. Each used 100 bootstrap fits, 60 independent noise trials and withheld later scans. BrainWeb inference also tested anatomy/grid mismatch. PAT16 prediction intervals additionally used \PPDraws{} predictive draws, Gaussian-noise integration and \PPTrials{} independent datasets with 100 nested bootstrap refits each. A separate PAT16 pilot compared trace-matched isotropic/directional transport in 32 assumed-parameter scenarios with measured static connectivity.\par
\noindent\textbf{Results.} On the 2 mm forward grid, coupling reduced day-90 viable count by \CellReduction\%, while low-threshold volume differed by \VolumeReduction\%. Localized necrosis and oxygen minima remained grid sensitive. Homogeneous rates lay inside bootstrap 95\% intervals. BrainWeb estimates were $(\BWWhiteEstimate,\BWGrayEstimate,\BWRhoEstimate)$ for known $(0.5,0.1,0.04)$; the true proliferation rate narrowly missed both interval types. Noise-free anatomy/grid mismatch shifted gray-matter diffusion by +\BWGrayStressShift\%. PAT16 $(D_{\rm WM},D_{\rm GM},\rho,r)$ estimates were $(\PRWhiteEstimate,\PRGrayEstimate,\PRRhoEstimate,\PRRatioEstimate)$ for known $(0.5,0.1,0.04,2.5)$, with all values inside both local and bootstrap intervals. Local-interval coverage was \PRWhiteCoverage/\PRTrials{}, \PRGrayCoverage/\PRTrials{}, \PRRhoCoverage/\PRTrials{} and \PRRatioCoverage/\PRTrials{}, respectively. Withheld day-90 signal RMSE was \PRDayNinetyRMSE{}; integrated nominal 95\% prediction intervals contained \PRDaySeventyFivePredictionContainment\% and \PRDayNinetyPredictionContainment\% of withheld observations at days 75/90. Repeated full-cube mean coverage was \PPMixFullSeventyFive\% and \PPMixFullNinety\%, with Monte Carlo 95\% limits \PPMixFullSeventyFiveCI\% and \PPMixFullNinetyCI\%. The original paired PAT16 pilot gave a median directional-minus-isotropic burden difference of $\PATBurdenDifference$ mL equivalents.\par
\noindent\textbf{Conclusions.} Oxygen feedback can alter modeled interiors despite similar threshold-defined extent. Conditional two-, three- and four-parameter recovery is feasible under known initialization and imaging assumptions, including joint tissue migration and anisotropy. Noise-based intervals omit anatomy, direction, initialization and model error. Synthetic recovery and MRI integration do not establish patient growth-rate recovery, biological validation or clinical forecasting performance.
\end{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} glioma; reaction--diffusion; oxygen; necrosis; BrainWeb; parameter identifiability; MRI; validation.

\subsection*{Study scope and declarations}
Phantom experiments are simulations. PAT16 reuses deidentified public BTC preoperative derivatives for anatomy, directions and static connectivity; growth observations are synthetic. No new human/animal experiment, patient growth-rate fit, treatment simulation or clinical validation was performed. Source MRI is not redistributed. BTC documents ethics and consent \citep{aerts2022}; applicable secondary-use requirements remain to be checked.
