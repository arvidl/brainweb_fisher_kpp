\begin{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{Background.} Fisher--Kolmogorov--Petrovskii--Piskunov (Fisher--KPP) models describe glioma spread and growth but do not explicitly represent oxygen limitation or necrotic material. We developed a minimal coupled extension and an interactive workflow to examine these assumptions.\par
\noindent\textbf{Methods.} Tissue-dependent Fisher--KPP, oxygen-limited growth and viable-tumor--oxygen--necrosis dynamics were compared on BrainWeb over 90 days with assigned biological parameters. Analytical checks, ablations and refinement assessed the numerical method. Two synthetic experiments estimated homogeneous diffusion/proliferation and separate white-/gray-matter diffusion with shared proliferation from blurred, voxel-averaged signals. Early scans calibrated each fit; later scans were withheld. Each experiment used 100 bootstrap fits and 60 independent noise trials. BrainWeb inference additionally tested a change in anatomy/grid resolution. A separate sub-PAT16 pilot used real preoperative T1, diffusion MRI and resting-state fMRI to compare trace-matched isotropic and directional transport in 32 paired assumed-parameter scenarios.\par
\noindent\textbf{Results.} On the 2 mm forward grid, coupling reduced day-90 viable count by \CellReduction\% versus Fisher--KPP, while low-threshold volume differed by \VolumeReduction\%. Growth inhibition dominated; localized necrosis and oxygen minima remained grid sensitive. Homogeneous rates were recovered within bootstrap 95\% intervals. In BrainWeb, known rates $(D_{\rm WM},D_{\rm GM},\rho)=(0.5,0.1,0.04)$ were estimated as $(\BWWhiteEstimate,\BWGrayEstimate,\BWRhoEstimate)$ in mm$^2$/day, mm$^2$/day and day$^{-1}$. The true proliferation rate fell just outside both 95\% intervals in the primary realization. Independent local-interval coverage was \BWWhiteCoverage/\BWTrials{}, \BWGrayCoverage/\BWTrials{} and \BWRhoCoverage/\BWTrials{}, respectively. Fitting noise-free 2 mm anatomy with the 3 mm model shifted gray-matter diffusion by +\BWGrayStressShift\%. In PAT16, the paired median anisotropic-minus-isotropic normalized day-90 burden was $\PATBurdenDifference$ mL equivalents, with 5th--95th sensitivity percentiles of $[-6.80,-0.85]$; measured connectivity was overlaid without predicting function.\par
\noindent\textbf{Conclusions.} Oxygen feedback can alter the modeled interior despite similar threshold-defined extent. Conditional two- and three-rate inference is feasible under known initialization and imaging assumptions, but noise-based intervals omit anatomy/discretization error. Translation requires calibrated observation operators, broader uncertainty assessment, independent biological targets and external forecasts. The MRI pilot demonstrates anatomical/directional integration, not patient growth-rate recovery or clinical validation.
\end{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} glioma; reaction--diffusion; oxygen; necrosis; BrainWeb; parameter identifiability; MRI; validation.

\subsection*{Study scope and declarations}
The BrainWeb and homogeneous experiments are computer simulations on phantoms. The separate PAT16 pilot reuses deidentified preoperative MRI derivatives from the public BTC study and its linked digital-twin workflow; no new human sampling or animal experiment was performed. Its anatomy, directions and static functional connectivity are measurement-derived, while growth rates and initial density are assumed. Parameter recovery used synthetic measurements only; no patient growth-rate fitting, treatment simulation or external biological validation was performed. Original data attribution and access terms apply; source MRI volumes are not redistributed in this repository. The original study's ethics and consent information is documented with the BTC dataset \citep{aerts2022}; applicable institutional requirements for secondary reuse must be checked before submission. Authorship, affiliations, funding, competing interests, contributions and any required AI-use declaration must be finalized before journal submission.
